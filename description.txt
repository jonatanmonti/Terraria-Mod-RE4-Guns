RE4 Weapons adds the full arsenal of Resident Evil 4 (2005): 18 weapons.
[c/C83C32:Handguns] - Handgun, Punisher, Red9, Blacktail, Matilda
[c/C83C32:Shotguns] - Shotgun, Riot Gun, Striker
[c/C83C32:Rifles] - Rifle, Rifle (semi-auto)
[c/C83C32:Machine guns] - TMP, Chicago Typewriter
[c/C83C32:Magnums] - Broken Butterfly, Killer7, Handcannon
[c/C83C32:Special] - Mine Thrower, Rocket Launcher, Knife

Guns use bullets (launchers use rockets). Craft them at an Anvil (or Mythril Anvil in Hardmode), buy them from the Arms Dealer as you progress, or find rare drops.

Agrega las 18 armas del Resident Evil 4 (2005). Incluye traducción al español.
